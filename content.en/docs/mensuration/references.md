Pappus, born at Alexandria in Egypt, was a great Greek geometer. His masterwork is the eight-book mathematical compilation called "Synagogue" (Collection).

Pappus describes solid shapes such as the right circular cylinder, cone, spheres and axes. Nature and modern technology continue to make use of such mathematical shapes.
