Mathematicians do not care to know about objects, but they want to reveal the relationship between them... the size of things does not matter, but they want to understand their form. — Henri Poincare

Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz (also known as von Leibniz) was a major German mathematician, philosopher, naturalist and inventor. He made extensive contributions across 26 subjects, including geology, medicine, biology, epidemiology, paleontology, psychology, engineering, linguistics, sociology, ethics, history, politics, law, and the theory of probability. The word "function", as used by Leibniz, referred to any quantity on a curve that varies from one point to another.
