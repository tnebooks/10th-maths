## If $A=\{a, b, p\}$, $B =\{2, 3\}$, $C =\{p, q, r, s\}$, then $n[(A \cup C) \times B]$ is :

---

#### Given $A=\{a, b, p\}$ and $C=\{p, q, r, s\}$.

Union $A \cup C = \{a, b, p, q, r, s\}$, so $n(A \cup C) = 6$.
Since $B = \{2, 3\}$, $n(B) = 2$.

Therefore, $n[(A \cup C) \times B] = n(A \cup C) \times n(B) = 6 \times 2 = 12$.
