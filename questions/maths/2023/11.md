## The height of a right circular cone whose radius is $5$ cm and slant height is $13$ cm will be :

---

#### In a right circular cone, the relationship between radius $r$, height $h$, and slant height $l$ is $l^2 = r^2 + h^2$.

Given $r = 5\text{ cm}$ and $l = 13\text{ cm}$:

<div class="math-display">$$h = \sqrt{l^2 - r^2} = \sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = \sqrt{169 - 25} = \sqrt{144} = 12\text{ cm}$$</div>
