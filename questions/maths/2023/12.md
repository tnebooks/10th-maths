## The ratio of the volumes of a cylinder, a cone and a sphere, if each has the same diameter and same height is :

---

#### Let the common diameter be $d = 2r$, so radius is $r$.
For a sphere of radius $r$, its height is equal to its diameter: $h = 2r$.
Thus, each solid has height $h = 2r$ and radius $r$:
- Volume of cylinder: $V_1 = \pi r^2 h = \pi r^2 (2r) = 2\pi r^3$
- Volume of cone: $V_2 = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 (2r) = \frac{2}{3}\pi r^3$
- Volume of sphere: $V_3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$

The ratio $V_1 : V_2 : V_3$ is:

<div class="math-display">$$2\pi r^3 : \frac{2}{3}\pi r^3 : \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$$</div>


Dividing each term by $\frac{2}{3}\pi r^3$ gives:

<div class="math-display">$$3 : 1 : 2$$</div>
