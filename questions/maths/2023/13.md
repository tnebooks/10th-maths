## If the sum and mean of a data are 407 and 11 respectively, then the number of observations in the data are :

---

#### The arithmetic mean $\bar{x}$ of a data set is given by:

<div class="math-display">$$\bar{x} = \frac{\sum x}{n}$$</div>


Given $\sum x = 407$ and $\bar{x} = 11$:

<div class="math-display">$$n = \frac{\sum x}{\bar{x}} = \frac{407}{11} = 37$$</div>
.

Therefore, the number of observations is 37.
