## If a letter is chosen at random from the English alphabets $\{a, b, \dots, z\}$, then the probability that the letter chosen precedes $x$ :

---

#### The total number of letters in the English alphabet is $n(S) = 26$.
The letter $x$ is the 24th letter in the alphabet.
The letters that precede $x$ are the first 23 letters ($a$ through $w$), so $n(E) = 23$.

The probability is:

<div class="math-display">$$P(E) = \frac{n(E)}{n(S)} = \frac{23}{26}$$</div>
.
