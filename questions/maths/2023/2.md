## If $n(A) = p$, $n(B) = q$, then the total number of relations that exist from $A$ to $B$ is :

---

#### The Cartesian product $A \times B$ has $n(A \times B) = n(A) \times n(B) = pq$ elements.
A relation from $A$ to $B$ is any subset of $A \times B$.
The total number of subsets of a set with $pq$ elements is $2^{pq}$.
Therefore, the total number of relations is $2^{pq}$.
