## $\frac{3y-3}{y} \div \frac{7y-7}{3y^2}$ is :

---

#### Convert division to multiplication by the reciprocal:

<div class="math-display">$$\frac{3y-3}{y} \div \frac{7y-7}{3y^2} = \frac{3(y-1)}{y} \times \frac{3y^2}{7(y-1)}$$</div>


Canceling common factor $(y-1)$ and simplifying $y$:

<div class="math-display">$$= \frac{3 \times 3y^2}{7y} = \frac{9y^2}{7y} = \frac{9y}{7}$$</div>
