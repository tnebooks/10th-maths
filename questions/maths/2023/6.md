## Graph of a Quadratic equation is a :

---

#### A quadratic equation in two variables has the standard form $y = ax^2 + bx + c$ ($a 
eq 0$). Its curve on a Cartesian coordinate plane is U-shaped (opening upwards or downwards), which is geometrically defined as a parabola.
