## If in triangles $ABC$ and $EDF$, $\frac{AB}{DE} = \frac{BC}{FD}$, then they will be similar, when :

---

#### By the SAS (Side-Angle-Side) similarity criterion, two triangles are similar if two pairs of corresponding sides are proportional and their included angles are equal.

In $\triangle ABC$, the sides in the ratio are $AB$ and $BC$, and the angle included between them is $\angle B$.
In $\triangle EDF$, the corresponding sides are $DE$ and $FD$, and the angle included between them is $\angle D$.

Therefore, the triangles are similar when $\angle B = \angle D$.
