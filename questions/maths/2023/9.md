## The slope of the straight line perpendicular to the x-axis is :

---

#### A straight line perpendicular to the x-axis is a vertical line parallel to the y-axis.
Its inclination angle with the positive x-axis is $\theta = 90^\circ$.

The slope is given by $m = \tan \theta = \tan 90^\circ = \infty$ (undefined).
