## If $n(A \times B) = 6$ and $A = \{1, 3\}$, then $n(B)$ is :

---

#### Given $A = \{1, 3\}$, the number of elements in $A$ is $n(A) = 2$.

We know that $n(A \times B) = n(A) \times n(B)$.

<div class="math-display">$$6 = 2 \times n(B) \implies n(B) = \frac{6}{2} = 3$$</div>
.

Thus, $n(B) = 3$.
