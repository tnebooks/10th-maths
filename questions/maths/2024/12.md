## The ratio of the volumes of a cylinder, a cone and a sphere, if each has the same diameter and same height is :

---

#### Let common radius be $r$ and height $h = 2r$ (height of sphere equals its diameter $2r$).
- Volume of cylinder: $V_1 = \pi r^2 (2r) = 2\pi r^3$
- Volume of cone: $V_2 = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 (2r) = \frac{2}{3}\pi r^3$
- Volume of sphere: $V_3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$

Ratio $V_1 : V_2 : V_3 = 2 : \frac{2}{3} : \frac{4}{3} = 3 : 1 : 2$.
