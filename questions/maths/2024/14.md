## The probability of getting a job for a person is $\frac{x}{3}$. If the probability of not getting the job is $\frac{2}{3}$, then the value of $x$ is :

---

#### The sum of the probability of an event and its complement is 1:

<div class="math-display">$$P(\text{job}) + P(\text{no job}) = 1$$</div>


<div class="math-display">$$\frac{x}{3} + \frac{2}{3} = 1 \implies \frac{x + 2}{3} = 1 \implies x + 2 = 3 \implies x = 1$$</div>
.

Thus, $x = 1$.
