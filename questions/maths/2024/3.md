## The least number that is divisible by all the numbers from 1 to 10 (both inclusive) is :

---

#### The least number divisible by all numbers from 1 to 10 is their Least Common Multiple (LCM):
- $1 = 1$, $2 = 2$, $3 = 3$, $4 = 2^2$, $5 = 5$, $6 = 2 \times 3$, $7 = 7$, $8 = 2^3$, $9 = 3^2$, $10 = 2 \times 5$


<div class="math-display">$$\text{LCM} = 2^3 \times 3^2 \times 5 \times 7 = 8 \times 9 \times 5 \times 7 = 2520$$</div>
