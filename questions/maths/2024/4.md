## An A.P. consists of 31 terms. If its 16th term is $m$, then the sum of all the terms of this A.P. is :

---

#### The sum of $n$ terms of an A.P. is $S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n-1)d] = n\left(a + \frac{n-1}{2}d\right)$.
For $n = 31$:

<div class="math-display">$$S_{31} = 31\left(a + \frac{31-1}{2}d\right) = 31(a + 15d)$$</div>


The 16th term of the A.P. is $t_{16} = a + (16-1)d = a + 15d = m$.
Therefore, $S_{31} = 31 \times m = 31m$.
