## If in $\triangle ABC$, $DE \parallel BC$, $AB = 3.6$ cm, $AC = 2.4$ cm and $AD = 2.1$ cm, then the length of $AE$ is :

---

#### By Thales Theorem (Basic Proportionality Theorem), since $DE \parallel BC$:

<div class="math-display">$$\frac{AD}{AB} = \frac{AE}{AC}$$</div>


Substituting the given values:

<div class="math-display">$$\frac{2.1}{3.6} = \frac{AE}{2.4} \implies AE = \frac{2.1 \times 2.4}{3.6} = \frac{2.1 \times 2}{3} = 0.7 \times 2 = 1.4\text{ cm}$$</div>
