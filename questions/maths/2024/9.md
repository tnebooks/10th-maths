## The area of triangle formed by the points $(-5, 0)$, $(0, -5)$ and $(5, 0)$ is :

---

#### The vertices are $A(-5, 0)$, $B(0, -5)$, and $C(5, 0)$.

Using the area formula $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} |x_1(y_2 - y_3) + x_2(y_3 - y_1) + x_3(y_1 - y_2)|$:

<div class="math-display">$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} |(-5)(-5 - 0) + 0(0 - 0) + 5(0 - (-5))| = \frac{1}{2} |25 + 0 + 25| = \frac{1}{2}(50) = 25\text{ sq. units}$$</div>
