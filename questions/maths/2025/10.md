## If the ratio of the height of a tower and the length of its shadow is $\sqrt{3} : 1$, then the angle of elevation of the sun has measure :

---

#### Let height of tower be $h$ and shadow length be $s$.
We are given $\frac{h}{s} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{1}$.

In the right-angled triangle formed, $\tan\theta = \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}} = \frac{h}{s} = \sqrt{3}$.
Since $\tan 60^\circ = \sqrt{3}$, the angle of elevation is $\theta = 60^\circ$.
