## The curved surface area of a right circular cone of height 15 cm and base diameter 16 cm is :

---

#### Diameter $d = 16\text{ cm} \implies$ radius $r = 8\text{ cm}$, and height $h = 15\text{ cm}$.

Slant height $l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2} = \sqrt{8^2 + 15^2} = \sqrt{64 + 225} = \sqrt{289} = 17\text{ cm}$.

Curved Surface Area $\text{CSA} = \pi r l = \pi \times 8 \times 17 = 136\pi\text{ cm}^2$.
