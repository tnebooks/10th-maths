## The volume (in cm$^3$) of the greatest sphere that can be cut off from a cylindrical log of wood of base radius 1 cm and height 5 cm is :

---

#### The diameter of the cylinder is $2r = 2(1) = 2\text{ cm}$.
Since the height of the cylinder is $5\text{ cm} > 2\text{ cm}$, the maximum diameter of the sphere that can be carved from the cylinder is $2\text{ cm}$, giving a radius $R = 1\text{ cm}$.

Volume of sphere $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi R^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi (1)^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi\text{ cm}^3$.
