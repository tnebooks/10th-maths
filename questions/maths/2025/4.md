## If 6 times the 6th term of an A.P. is equal to 7 times the 7th term, then the 13th term of the A.P. is :

---

#### Given $6 \cdot t_6 = 7 \cdot t_7$:

<div class="math-display">$$6(a + 5d) = 7(a + 6d)$$</div>


<div class="math-display">$$6a + 30d = 7a + 42d$$</div>


<div class="math-display">$$7a - 6a + 42d - 30d = 0 \implies a + 12d = 0$$</div>


The 13th term is $t_{13} = a + (13 - 1)d = a + 12d = 0$.
