## If $(x - 6)$ is the H.C.F of $x^2 - 2x - 24$ and $x^2 - kx - 6$, then the value of $k$ is :

---

#### Since $(x - 6)$ is a factor of $x^2 - kx - 6$, by the factor theorem substituting $x = 6$ yields 0:

<div class="math-display">$$6^2 - k(6) - 6 = 0$$</div>


<div class="math-display">$$36 - 6k - 6 = 0 \implies 30 - 6k = 0 \implies 6k = 30 \implies k = 5$$</div>
