## The equation of a line passing through the origin and parallel to the line $7x - 3y + 4 = 0$ is :

---

#### Any line parallel to $ax + by + c = 0$ has the form $ax + by + k = 0$.
Here, the line is parallel to $7x - 3y + 4 = 0$, so its equation is $7x - 3y + k = 0$.
Since it passes through the origin $(0, 0)$:

<div class="math-display">$$7(0) - 3(0) + k = 0 \implies k = 0$$</div>

Thus, the required equation is $7x - 3y = 0$.
