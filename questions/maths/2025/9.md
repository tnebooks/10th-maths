## The slope of the line which is perpendicular to a line joining the points $(0, 0)$ and $(-8, 8)$ is :

---

#### The slope $m_1$ of the line segment joining $(0, 0)$ and $(-8, 8)$ is:

<div class="math-display">$$m_1 = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{8 - 0}{-8 - 0} = \frac{8}{-8} = -1$$</div>


For two perpendicular lines, $m_1 \times m_2 = -1$:

<div class="math-display">$$(-1) \times m_2 = -1 \implies m_2 = 1$$</div>
