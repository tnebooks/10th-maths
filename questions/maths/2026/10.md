## If $\tan\theta + \cot\theta = 2$, then $\tan^2\theta + \cot^2\theta$ is equal to :

---

#### Squaring both sides of $\tan\theta + \cot\theta = 2$:

<div class="math-display">$$(\tan\theta + \cot\theta)^2 = 2^2$$</div>


<div class="math-display">$$\tan^2\theta + \cot^2\theta + 2\tan\theta\cot\theta = 4$$</div>


Since $\cot\theta = \frac{1}{\tan\theta}$, we have $\tan\theta\cot\theta = 1$:

<div class="math-display">$$\tan^2\theta + \cot^2\theta + 2(1) = 4 \implies \tan^2\theta + \cot^2\theta = 4 - 2 = 2$$</div>
