## A solid sphere of radius $x$ cm is melted and cast into a shape of a solid cone of same radius. The height of the cone is :

---

#### When a solid is melted and recast, its volume is conserved:

<div class="math-display">$$\text{Volume of solid sphere} = \text{Volume of solid cone}$$</div>


<div class="math-display">$$\frac{4}{3}\pi x^3 = \frac{1}{3}\pi x^2 h$$</div>


Dividing both sides by $\frac{1}{3}\pi x^2$:

<div class="math-display">$$4x = h \implies h = 4x\text{ cm}$$</div>
