## A spherical ball of radius $r_1$ units is melted to make 8 new identical balls each of radius $r_2$ units. Then $r_1 : r_2$ is :

---

#### Volume of original spherical ball $= \frac{4}{3}\pi r_1^3$.
Volume of 8 identical new balls $= 8 \times \left(\frac{4}{3}\pi r_2^3\right) = \frac{4}{3}\pi (8r_2^3)$.

Equating volumes:

<div class="math-display">$$r_1^3 = 8r_2^3 \implies \frac{r_1^3}{r_2^3} = 8 \implies \frac{r_1}{r_2} = \sqrt[3]{8} = 2$$</div>


Thus, the ratio $r_1 : r_2 = 2 : 1$.
