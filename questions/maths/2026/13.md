## For a collection of data, $\sum x^2 = 140$, $\sum x = 28$ and $n = 7$, the standard deviation is :

---

#### The formula for standard deviation $\sigma$ is:

<div class="math-display">$$\sigma = \sqrt{\frac{\sum x^2}{n} - \left(\frac{\sum x}{n}\right)^2}$$</div>


Calculate terms:
- $\frac{\sum x^2}{n} = \frac{140}{7} = 20$
- $\frac{\sum x}{n} = \frac{28}{7} = 4 \implies \left(\frac{\sum x}{n}\right)^2 = 4^2 = 16$


<div class="math-display">$$\sigma = \sqrt{20 - 16} = \sqrt{4} = 2$$</div>
