## The probability of a red marble selected at random from a jar containing $p$ red, $q$ blue and $r$ green marbles is :

---

#### Total number of marbles in the jar $n(S) = p + q + r$.
Number of red marbles $n(E) = p$.

Probability of choosing a red marble $P(E) = \frac{n(E)}{n(S)} = \frac{p}{p + q + r}$.
