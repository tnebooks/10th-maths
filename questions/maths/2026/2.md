## $f(x) = (x + 1)^3 - (x - 1)^3$ represents a function which is :

---

#### Expanding both cube terms:
- $(x + 1)^3 = x^3 + 3x^2 + 3x + 1$
- $(x - 1)^3 = x^3 - 3x^2 + 3x - 1$

Subtracting the two expressions:

<div class="math-display">$$f(x) = (x^3 + 3x^2 + 3x + 1) - (x^3 - 3x^2 + 3x - 1) = 6x^2 + 2$$</div>


Since the highest degree of $x$ is 2, $f(x)$ represents a quadratic function.
