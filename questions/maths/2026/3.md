## If the H.C.F of 65 and 117 is expressible in the form of $65m - 117$, then the value of $m$ is :

---

#### Prime factorize both numbers:
- $65 = 5 \times 13$
- $117 = 3^2 \times 13$

Thus, $\text{HCF}(65, 117) = 13$.
Given $65m - 117 = 13$:

<div class="math-display">$$65m = 13 + 117 = 130 \implies m = \frac{130}{65} = 2$$</div>
