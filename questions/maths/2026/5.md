## The solution of the system $x + y - 3z = -6$, $-7y + 7z = 7$, $3z = 9$ is :

---

#### 1. From $3z = 9 \implies z = 3$.
2. Substitute $z = 3$ into $-7y + 7(3) = 7 \implies -7y + 21 = 7 \implies -7y = -14 \implies y = 2$.
3. Substitute $y = 2$ and $z = 3$ into $x + y - 3z = -6$:

<div class="math-display">$$x + 2 - 3(3) = -6 \implies x - 7 = -6 \implies x = 1$$</div>
.

Thus, $x = 1, y = 2, z = 3$.
