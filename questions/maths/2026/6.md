## For the matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 3 & 5 & 7 \\ 2 & 4 & 6 & 8 \\ 9 & 11 & 13 & 15 \end{pmatrix}$, the order of the transpose matrix $A^T$ is :

---

#### The matrix $A$ has 3 rows and 4 columns, so its order is $3 \times 4$.
The transpose of an $m \times n$ matrix has order $n \times m$.
Therefore, the order of $A^T$ is $4 \times 3$.
