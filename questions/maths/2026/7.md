## Two poles of heights 6 m and 11 m stand vertically on a plane ground. If the distance between their feet is 12 m, what is the distance between their tops ?

---

#### Let the vertical poles be $AB = 6\text{ m}$ and $CD = 11\text{ m}$, with feet distance $BD = 12\text{ m}$.
Draw a horizontal line $AE \perp CD$ with $E$ on $CD$.
Then $DE = AB = 6\text{ m}$ and $AE = BD = 12\text{ m}$.
The remaining vertical height $CE = CD - DE = 11 - 6 = 5\text{ m}$.

By Pythagoras theorem in right triangle $\triangle AEC$:

<div class="math-display">$$AC = \sqrt{AE^2 + CE^2} = \sqrt{12^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{144 + 25} = \sqrt{169} = 13\text{ m}$$</div>
.
