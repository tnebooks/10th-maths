## The point of intersection of the straight lines $3x - y = 4$ and $x + y = 8$ is :

---

#### Given system:
1) $3x - y = 4$
2) $x + y = 8$

Adding the two equations eliminates $y$:

<div class="math-display">$$4x = 12 \implies x = 3$$</div>


Substituting $x = 3$ into equation (2):

<div class="math-display">$$3 + y = 8 \implies y = 5$$</div>


Thus, the point of intersection is $(3, 5)$.
