## The values of $a$ and $b$ if $4x^4-24x^3+76x^2+ax+b$ is a perfect square are

---

#### Assume the square root is $(2x^2 + px + q)$. Expanding gives $4x^4 + 4px^3 + (p^2+4q)x^2 + 2pqx + q^2$. Comparing coefficients: $4p = -24 \implies p = -6$. Then $p^2+4q = 76 \implies (-6)^2+4q = 76 \implies 36+4q=76 \implies 4q=40 \implies q = 10$. Finally, $a = 2pq = 2(-6)(10) = -120$ and $b = q^2 = 10^2 = 100$.
