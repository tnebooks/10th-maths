## The number of points of intersection of the quadratic polynomial $x^2+4x+4$ with the X axis is

---

#### For the polynomial $x^2+4x+4$, rewrite it as $(x+2)^2 = 0$. The discriminant is $\Delta = b^2 - 4ac = 4^2 - 4(1)(4) = 16 - 16 = 0$. When $\Delta = 0$, the quadratic equation has real and equal roots ($x = -2$). Hence, the parabola touches the X-axis at exactly 1 point, $(-2, 0)$.
