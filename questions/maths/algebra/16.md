## If number of columns and rows are not equal in a matrix then it is said to be a

---

#### A matrix with $m$ rows and $n$ columns where $m \ne n$ is called a rectangular matrix. Diagonal, square, and identity matrices all require $m = n$ (equal rows and columns).
