## Find the matrix $X$ if $2X+\begin{pmatrix}1&3\\5&7\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}5&7\\9&5\end{pmatrix}$.

---

#### Subtracting the matrix from both sides gives $2X = \begin{pmatrix}5&7\\9&5\end{pmatrix} - \begin{pmatrix}1&3\\5&7\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix}5-1 & 7-3 \\ 9-5 & 5-7\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix}4&4\\4&-2\end{pmatrix}$. Dividing each entry by 2 gives $X = \begin{pmatrix}2&2\\2&-1\end{pmatrix}$.
