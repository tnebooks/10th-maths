## If $(x-6)$ is the HCF of $x^2-2x-24$ and $x^2-kx-6$ then the value of $k$ is

---

#### Since $(x-6)$ is the HCF, $(x-6)$ must be a factor of $x^2-kx-6$. By the factor theorem, substituting $x=6$ into the polynomial yields zero: $6^2 - k(6) - 6 = 0 \implies 36 - 6k - 6 = 0 \implies 30 - 6k = 0 \implies 6k = 30 \implies k = 5$.
