## $\frac{3y-3}{y}\div\frac{7y-7}{3y^2}$ is

---

#### Convert division to multiplication by multiplying by the reciprocal: $\frac{3(y-1)}{y} \times \frac{3y^2}{7(y-1)}$. Canceling the common non-zero factor $(y-1)$ gives $\frac{3 \times 3y^2}{7y} = \frac{9y^2}{7y} = \frac{9y}{7}$.
