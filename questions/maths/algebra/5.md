## $y^2+\frac1{y^2}$ is not equal to

---

#### Expanding $\left(y+\frac{1}{y}\right)^2$ gives $y^2 + 2(y)\left(\frac{1}{y}\right) + \frac{1}{y^2} = y^2 + 2 + \frac{1}{y^2} \ne y^2 + \frac{1}{y^2}$. On the other hand, $\frac{y^4+1}{y^2} = y^2+\frac{1}{y^2}$, $\left(y-\frac{1}{y}\right)^2+2 = y^2-2+\frac{1}{y^2}+2 = y^2+\frac{1}{y^2}$, and $\left(y+\frac{1}{y}\right)^2-2 = y^2+2+\frac{1}{y^2}-2 = y^2+\frac{1}{y^2}$. Hence, it is not equal to $\left(y+\frac{1}{y}\right)^2$.
