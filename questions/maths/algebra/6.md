## $\frac{x}{x^2-25}-\frac8{x^2+6x+5}$ gives

---

#### Factor the denominators: $x^2-25 = (x-5)(x+5)$ and $x^2+6x+5 = (x+5)(x+1)$. The common denominator is $(x-5)(x+5)(x+1) = (x^2-25)(x+1)$. Combining the numerators gives $x(x+1) - 8(x-5) = x^2 + x - 8x + 40 = x^2 - 7x + 40$. Thus, the expression simplifies to $\frac{x^2-7x+40}{(x^2-25)(x+1)}$.
