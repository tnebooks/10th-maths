## The square root of $\frac{256x^8y^4z^{10}}{25x^6y^6z^6}$ is equal to

---

#### First simplify the terms under the square root: $\frac{256}{25} \cdot \frac{x^8}{x^6} \cdot \frac{y^4}{y^6} \cdot \frac{z^{10}}{z^6} = \frac{256}{25} \cdot \frac{x^2 z^4}{y^2}$. Taking the square root gives $\sqrt{\frac{256}{25}\frac{x^2 z^4}{y^2}} = \frac{16}{5}\left|\frac{xz^2}{y}\right|$.
