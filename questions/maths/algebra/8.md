## Which of the following should be added to make $x^4+64$ a perfect square

---

#### Express $x^4 + 64$ as $a^2 + b^2$, where $a = x^2$ and $b = 8$. To make it a perfect square $(a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$, we must add the middle term $2ab = 2(x^2)(8) = 16x^2$. Adding $16x^2$ yields $x^4 + 16x^2 + 64 = (x^2+8)^2$.
