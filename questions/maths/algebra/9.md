## The solution of $(2x-1)^2=9$ is equal to

---

#### Taking square roots on both sides gives $2x - 1 = \pm\sqrt{9} = \pm 3$. Case 1: $2x - 1 = 3 \implies 2x = 4 \implies x = 2$. Case 2: $2x - 1 = -3 \implies 2x = -2 \implies x = -1$. Hence, the solutions are $-1, 2$.
