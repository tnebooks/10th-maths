## The equation of a line passing through the origin and perpendicular to the line $7x-3y+4=0$ is

---

#### The equation of any line perpendicular to $ax + by + c = 0$ is of the form $bx - ay + k = 0$.
For the line $7x - 3y + 4 = 0$, $a = 7$ and $b = -3$.
So, the perpendicular line is:
$$-3x - 7y + k = 0 \implies 3x + 7y = k$$
Since it passes through the origin $(0,0)$:
$$3(0) + 7(0) = k \implies k = 0$$
Therefore, the required equation is $3x + 7y = 0$.
