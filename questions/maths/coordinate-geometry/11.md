## Consider four straight lines

(i) $l_1$; $3y=4x+5$

(ii) $l_2$; $4y=3x-1$

(iii) $l_3$; $4y+3x=7$

(iv) $l_4$; $4x+3y=2$

Which of the following statement is true?

---

#### Find the slope of each line by expressing in $y = mx + c$ form:
- $l_1: y = \frac{4}{3}x + \frac{5}{3} \implies m_1 = \frac{4}{3}$
- $l_2: y = \frac{3}{4}x - \frac{1}{4} \implies m_2 = \frac{3}{4}$
- $l_3: y = -\frac{3}{4}x + \frac{7}{4} \implies m_3 = -\frac{3}{4}$
- $l_4: y = -\frac{4}{3}x + \frac{2}{3} \implies m_4 = -\frac{4}{3}$

Now test the product of slopes for $l_2$ and $l_4$:
$$m_2 \times m_4 = \frac{3}{4} \times \left(-\frac{4}{3}\right) = -1$$
Since $m_2 \times m_4 = -1$, the lines $l_2$ and $l_4$ are perpendicular.
