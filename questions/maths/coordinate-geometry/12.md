## A straight line has equation $8y=4x+21$. Which of the following is true

---

#### Convert the equation $8y = 4x + 21$ to slope-intercept form $y = mx + c$ by dividing both sides by 8:
$$y = \frac{4}{8}x + \frac{21}{8} = 0.5x + 2.625$$
Therefore, the slope is $m = 0.5$ and the $y$-intercept is approximately $2.6$.
