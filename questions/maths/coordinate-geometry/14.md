## When proving that a quadrilateral is a parallelogram by using slopes you must find

---

#### A quadrilateral is a parallelogram if both pairs of opposite sides are parallel. Using slopes, two lines are parallel if their slopes are equal. Therefore, one must find the slopes of two pairs of opposite sides and show that the slopes of opposite sides are equal.
