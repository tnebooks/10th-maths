## $(2,1)$ is the point of intersection of two lines.

---

#### Substitute $(x,y) = (2,1)$ into the equations:
For $x + y = 3$:
$$2 + 1 = 3 \quad \text{(Satisfied)}$$
For $3x + y = 7$:
$$3(2) + 1 = 6 + 1 = 7 \quad \text{(Satisfied)}$$
Since $(2,1)$ satisfies both equations, it is their point of intersection.
