## A man walks near a wall, such that the distance between him and the wall is 10 units. Consider the wall to be the $Y$ axis. The path travelled by the man is

---

#### The wall is represented by the $Y$-axis, whose equation is $x = 0$.
The distance of any point $(x,y)$ from the $Y$-axis is given by $|x|$.
Since the distance between the man and the wall is always maintained at 10 units, $|x| = 10$, which gives $x = 10$ (or $x = -10$). Among the given options, the path is $x = 10$.
