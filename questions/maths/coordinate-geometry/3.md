## The straight line given by the equation $x=11$ is

---

#### An equation of the form $x = c$ (where $c$ is a constant) represents a vertical straight line that is parallel to the $Y$-axis and at a distance of $|c|$ units from it. Therefore, $x = 11$ is parallel to the $Y$-axis.
