## If $(5,7)$, $(3,p)$ and $(6,6)$ are collinear, then the value of $p$ is

---

#### Three points $A(5,7)$, $B(3,p)$, and $C(6,6)$ are collinear if the slope of $AB$ equals the slope of $AC$:
$$\text{Slope of } AC = \frac{6-7}{6-5} = \frac{-1}{1} = -1$$
$$\text{Slope of } AB = \frac{p-7}{3-5} = \frac{p-7}{-2}$$
Equating the slopes:
$$\frac{p-7}{-2} = -1 \implies p - 7 = 2 \implies p = 9$$
