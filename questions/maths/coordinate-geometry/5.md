## The point of intersection of $3x-y=4$ and $x+y=8$ is

---

#### Given the system of linear equations:
1) $3x - y = 4$
2) $x + y = 8$

Adding equations (1) and (2):
$$(3x - y) + (x + y) = 4 + 8 \implies 4x = 12 \implies x = 3$$
Substituting $x = 3$ into equation (2):
$$3 + y = 8 \implies y = 5$$
Thus, the point of intersection is $(3,5)$.
