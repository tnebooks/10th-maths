## The slope of the line joining $(12,3)$, $(4,a)$ is $\frac{1}{8}$. The value of '$a$' is

---

#### The slope of the line joining $(x_1,y_1) = (12,3)$ and $(x_2,y_2) = (4,a)$ is:
$$m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{a - 3}{4 - 12} = \frac{a - 3}{-8}$$
Given that the slope is $\frac{1}{8}$:
$$\frac{a - 3}{-8} = \frac{1}{8} \implies a - 3 = -1 \implies a = 2$$
