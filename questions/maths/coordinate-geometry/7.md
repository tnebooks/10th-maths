## The slope of the line which is perpendicular to a line joining the points $(0,0)$ and $(-8,8)$ is

---

#### The slope of the line joining $(0,0)$ and $(-8,8)$ is:
$$m_1 = \frac{8 - 0}{-8 - 0} = \frac{8}{-8} = -1$$
If two non-vertical lines are perpendicular, the product of their slopes is $-1$:
$$m_1 \times m_2 = -1 \implies (-1) \times m_2 = -1 \implies m_2 = 1$$
