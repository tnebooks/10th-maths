## If slope of the line $PQ$ is $\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$ then slope of the perpendicular bisector of $PQ$ is

---

#### The perpendicular bisector of $PQ$ is perpendicular to the line segment $PQ$.
For two perpendicular lines with slopes $m_1$ and $m_2$, the relation is:
$$m_1 \times m_2 = -1$$
Given $m_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$:
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \times m_2 = -1 \implies m_2 = -\sqrt{3}$$
