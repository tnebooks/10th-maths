## If $A$ is a point on the $Y$ axis whose ordinate is 8 and $B$ is a point on the $X$ axis whose abscissae is 5 then the equation of the line $AB$ is

---

#### Since point $A$ lies on the $Y$-axis with ordinate 8, its coordinates are $(0,8)$, which gives the $y$-intercept $b = 8$.
Since point $B$ lies on the $X$-axis with abscissa 5, its coordinates are $(5,0)$, which gives the $x$-intercept $a = 5$.
Using the intercept form of a line equation:
$$\frac{x}{a} + \frac{y}{b} = 1 \implies \frac{x}{5} + \frac{y}{8} = 1$$
Multiplying through by 40:
$$8x + 5y = 40$$
