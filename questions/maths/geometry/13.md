## The two tangents from an external points $P$ to a circle with centre at $O$ are $PA$ and $PB$. If $\angle APB=70^\circ$ then the value of $\angle AOB$ is

---

#### The radii $OA$ and $OB$ drawn to the points of contact are perpendicular to the tangents $PA$ and $PB$, so $\angle OAP = 90^\circ$ and $\angle OBP = 90^\circ$.
In quadrilateral $OAPB$, the sum of all interior angles is $360^\circ$:
$$\angle AOB + \angle OAP + \angle APB + \angle OBP = 360^\circ$$
$$\angle AOB + 90^\circ + 70^\circ + 90^\circ = 360^\circ \implies \angle AOB + 250^\circ = 360^\circ \implies \angle AOB = 110^\circ$$
Equivalently, the angle between the tangents and the angle subtended by the radii at the centre are supplementary: $\angle AOB = 180^\circ - 70^\circ = 110^\circ$.
