## In figure if $PR$ is tangent to the circle at $P$ and $O$ is the centre of the circle, then $\angle POQ$ is

![Exercise 4.5, Q15](assets/image-4-p41-2.png)

---

#### The radius $OP$ is perpendicular to tangent $PR$ at the point of contact $P$:
$$\angle OPR = 90^\circ$$
From the figure, the angle between chord $PQ$ and tangent $PR$ is $\angle QPR = 60^\circ$. Therefore:
$$\angle OPQ = \angle OPR - \angle QPR = 90^\circ - 60^\circ = 30^\circ$$
Since $OP = OQ$ are both radii of the same circle, $\triangle OPQ$ is an isosceles triangle:
$$\angle OQP = \angle OPQ = 30^\circ$$
In $\triangle OPQ$, the sum of angles is $180^\circ$:
$$\angle POQ = 180^\circ - (\angle OPQ + \angle OQP) = 180^\circ - (30^\circ + 30^\circ) = 180^\circ - 60^\circ = 120^\circ$$
