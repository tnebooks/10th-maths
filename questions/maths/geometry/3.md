## If $\triangle ABC$ is an isosceles triangle with $\angle C=90^\circ$ and $AC=5$ cm, then $AB$ is

---

#### Since $\triangle ABC$ is an isosceles right-angled triangle with right angle at $C$ ($\angle C = 90^\circ$), the two legs adjacent to the right angle are equal in length:
$$BC = AC = 5\text{ cm}$$
By the Pythagorean theorem, the hypotenuse $AB$ satisfies:
$$AB^2 = AC^2 + BC^2 = 5^2 + 5^2 = 25 + 25 = 50$$
$$AB = \sqrt{50} = \sqrt{25 \times 2} = 5\sqrt{2}\text{ cm}$$
