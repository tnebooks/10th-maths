## The perimeters of two similar triangles $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle PQR$ are $36$ cm and $24$ cm respectively. If $PQ=10$ cm, then the length of $AB$ is

---

#### The ratio of the perimeters of two similar triangles is equal to the ratio of their corresponding sides:
$$\dfrac{\text{Perimeter}(\triangle ABC)}{\text{Perimeter}(\triangle PQR)} = \dfrac{AB}{PQ}$$
Substituting the given values:
$$\dfrac{36}{24} = \dfrac{AB}{10} \implies \dfrac{3}{2} = \dfrac{AB}{10} \implies AB = \dfrac{3 \times 10}{2} = 15\text{ cm}$$
