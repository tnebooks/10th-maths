## If in $\triangle ABC$, $DE\parallel BC$. $AB=3.6$ cm, $AC=2.4$ cm and $AD=2.1$ cm then the length of AE is

---

#### By Thales Theorem (Basic Proportionality Theorem), a line drawn parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two sides proportionally:
$$\dfrac{AD}{AB} = \dfrac{AE}{AC}$$
Substituting the given values:
$$\dfrac{2.1}{3.6} = \dfrac{AE}{2.4} \implies AE = \dfrac{2.1 \times 2.4}{3.6} = \dfrac{2.1 \times 2}{3} = 0.7 \times 2 = 1.4\text{ cm}$$
