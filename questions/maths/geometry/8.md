## In the adjacent figure $\angle BAC=90^\circ$ and $AD\perp BC$ then

![Exercise 4.5, Q8](assets/image-4-p40-2.png)

---

#### In right-angled triangle $\triangle ABC$ with $\angle BAC = 90^\circ$ and altitude $AD \perp BC$, the triangles on either side of the altitude are similar to each other:
$$\triangle DBA \sim \triangle DAC$$
Since corresponding sides are in proportion:
$$\dfrac{BD}{AD} = \dfrac{AD}{CD} \implies AD^2 = BD \cdot CD \implies BD \cdot CD = AD^2$$
