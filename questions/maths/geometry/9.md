## Two poles of heights $6$ m and $11$ m stand vertically on a plane ground. If the distance between their feet is $12$ m, what is the distance between their tops?

---

#### Let the two vertical poles be $AB = 11\text{ m}$ and $CD = 6\text{ m}$ with feet separated by $BD = 12\text{ m}$.
Draw a horizontal line segment from $C$ perpendicular to $AB$ meeting $AB$ at $E$.
Then $CDBE$ forms a rectangle, which gives:
$$BE = CD = 6\text{ m} \quad \text{and} \quad CE = BD = 12\text{ m}$$
The remaining vertical height of the taller pole is:
$$AE = AB - BE = 11 - 6 = 5\text{ m}$$
In right-angled triangle $\triangle AEC$, the distance between their tops is hypotenuse $AC$:
$$AC^2 = AE^2 + CE^2 = 5^2 + 12^2 = 25 + 144 = 169 \implies AC = \sqrt{169} = 13\text{ m}$$
