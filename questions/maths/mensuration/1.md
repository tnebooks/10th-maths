## The curved surface area of a right circular cone of height $15$ cm and base diameter $16$ cm is

---

#### Given height $h = 15\text{ cm}$ and diameter $d = 16\text{ cm}$, the base radius is $r = \frac{16}{2} = 8\text{ cm}$.
The slant height $l$ of the cone is:
$$l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2} = \sqrt{8^2 + 15^2} = \sqrt{64 + 225} = \sqrt{289} = 17\text{ cm}$$
The curved surface area (CSA) of the cone is:
$$\text{CSA} = \pi r l = \pi \times 8 \times 17 = 136\pi\text{ cm}^2$$
