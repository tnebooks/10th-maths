## A frustum of a right circular cone is of height $16$cm with radii of its ends as $8$cm and $20$cm. Then, the volume of the frustum is

---

#### The formula for the volume of a frustum of height $h$ with radii $R$ and $r$ is:
$$V = \frac{1}{3}\pi h (R^2 + r^2 + Rr)$$
Substituting $h = 16\text{ cm}$, $R = 20\text{ cm}$, and $r = 8\text{ cm}$:
$$V = \frac{1}{3}\pi (16)(20^2 + 8^2 + 20 \times 8) = \frac{16\pi}{3}(400 + 64 + 160) = \frac{16\pi}{3}(624)$$
Since $624 / 3 = 208$:
$$V = 16 \times 208 \times \pi = 3328\pi\text{ cm}^3$$
