## A shuttle cock used for playing badminton has the shape of the combination of

---

#### A badminton shuttlecock consists of a skirt of feathers or nylon forming the flared frustum of a cone, attached to a cork base which is shaped as a hemisphere. Thus, it is a combination of a frustum of a cone and a hemisphere.
