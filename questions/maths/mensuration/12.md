## A spherical ball of radius $r_1$ units is melted to make $8$ new identical balls each of radius $r_2$ units. Then $r_1 : r_2$ is

---

#### The volume of the original spherical ball equals the sum of the volumes of the 8 smaller identical balls:
$$\frac{4}{3}\pi r_1^3 = 8 \times \left(\frac{4}{3}\pi r_2^3\right)$$
Dividing by $\frac{4}{3}\pi$:
$$r_1^3 = 8r_2^3 \implies \left(\frac{r_1}{r_2}\right)^3 = 8 \implies \frac{r_1}{r_2} = \sqrt[3]{8} = 2 = \frac{2}{1}$$
Thus, $r_1 : r_2 = 2:1$.
