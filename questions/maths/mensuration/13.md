## The volume (in cm$^3$) of the greatest sphere that can be cut off from a cylindrical log of wood of base radius $1$ cm and height $5$ cm is

---

#### The cylindrical log has diameter $2r = 2(1) = 2\text{ cm}$ and height $h = 5\text{ cm}$.
The maximum diameter of a sphere that can be carved from the cylinder is limited by the diameter of the cylinder ($2\text{ cm}$), since $2\text{ cm} < 5\text{ cm}$.
Thus, the radius of the greatest sphere is $R = 1\text{ cm}$.
Its volume is:
$$V = \frac{4}{3}\pi R^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi (1)^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi\text{ cm}^3$$
