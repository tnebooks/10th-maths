## If two solid hemispheres of same base radius r units are joined together along their bases, then curved surface area of this new solid is

---

#### When two identical hemispheres each of radius $r$ are joined base-to-base, they form a complete solid sphere of radius $r$.
The curved surface area of each hemisphere is $2\pi r^2$. When joined along their flat circular bases, those bases become internal, leaving only the two hemispherical curved surfaces exposed:
$$\text{Total Curved Surface Area} = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi r^2 = 4\pi r^2\text{ sq. units}$$
