## If the radius of the base of a right circular cylinder is halved keeping the same height, then the ratio of the volume of the cylinder thus obtained to the volume of original cylinder is

---

#### Let the original cylinder have base radius $r$ and height $h$. Its volume is:
$$V_1 = \pi r^2 h$$
The new cylinder has radius $r' = \frac{r}{2}$ and the same height $h$. Its volume is:
$$V_2 = \pi (r')^2 h = \pi \left(\frac{r}{2}\right)^2 h = \frac{1}{4}\pi r^2 h = \frac{1}{4}V_1$$
The ratio of the new volume to the original volume is:
$$\frac{V_2}{V_1} = \frac{\frac{1}{4}V_1}{V_1} = \frac{1}{4} = 1:4$$
