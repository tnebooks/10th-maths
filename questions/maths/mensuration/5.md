## The total surface area of a cylinder whose radius is $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of its height is

---

#### Given $r = \frac{h}{3}$. The total surface area (TSA) of a cylinder is:
$$\text{TSA} = 2\pi r(h + r)$$
Substituting $r = \frac{h}{3}$:
$$\text{TSA} = 2\pi \left(\frac{h}{3}\right) \left(h + \frac{h}{3}\right) = \frac{2\pi h}{3} \left(\frac{4h}{3}\right) = \frac{8\pi h^2}{9}\text{ sq.units}$$
