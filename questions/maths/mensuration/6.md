## In a hollow cylinder, the sum of the external and internal radii is $14$ cm and the width is $4$ cm. If its height is $20$ cm, the volume of the material in it is

---

#### Let $R$ and $r$ be the external and internal radii. We are given:
$$R + r = 14\text{ cm}, \quad R - r = 4\text{ cm}, \quad h = 20\text{ cm}$$
The volume of material in a hollow cylinder is:
$$V = \pi(R^2 - r^2)h = \pi(R + r)(R - r)h = \pi \times 14 \times 4 \times 20 = 1120\pi\text{ cm}^3$$
