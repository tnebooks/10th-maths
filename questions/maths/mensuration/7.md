## If the radius of the base of a cone is tripled and the height is doubled then the volume is

---

#### Original volume of the cone is $V_1 = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$.
When radius is tripled ($r' = 3r$) and height is doubled ($h' = 2h$), the new volume is:
$$V_2 = \frac{1}{3}\pi (r')^2 h' = \frac{1}{3}\pi (3r)^2 (2h) = \frac{1}{3}\pi (9r^2)(2h) = 18 \left(\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h\right) = 18 V_1$$
Therefore, the volume is made 18 times.
