## The total surface area of a hemi-sphere is how much times the square of its radius.

---

#### The total surface area of a hemisphere of radius $r$ consists of its curved surface area ($2\pi r^2$) plus its flat circular base area ($\pi r^2$):
$$\text{TSA} = 2\pi r^2 + \pi r^2 = 3\pi r^2 = 3\pi \times r^2$$
Thus, the total surface area is $3\pi$ times the square of its radius.
