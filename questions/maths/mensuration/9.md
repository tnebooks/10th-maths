## A solid sphere of radius $x$ cm is melted and cast into a shape of a solid cone of same radius. The height of the cone is

---

#### Since the sphere is melted and recast into a cone, their volumes are equal:
$$\text{Volume of sphere} = \text{Volume of cone}$$
$$\frac{4}{3}\pi x^3 = \frac{1}{3}\pi x^2 h$$
Multiplying both sides by 3 and dividing by $\pi x^2$:
$$4x = h \implies h = 4x\text{ cm}$$
