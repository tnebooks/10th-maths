## Euclid's division lemma states that for positive integers $a$ and $b$, there exist unique integers $q$ and $r$ such that $a = bq + r$, where $r$ must satisfy.

---

#### According to Euclid's division lemma, when a positive integer $a$ is divided by a positive integer $b$, the remainder $r$ must be non-negative and strictly less than the divisor $b$. Hence, $0 \leq r < b$.
