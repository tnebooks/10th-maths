## An A.P. consists of $31$ terms. If its $16^{\text{th}}$ term is $m$, then the sum of all the terms of this A.P. is

---

#### The sum of $n$ terms of an A.P. is $S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n-1)d]$. For $n = 31$:
$S_{31} = \frac{31}{2}[2a + 30d] = 31(a + 15d)$.
Since the $16^{\text{th}}$ term is $t_{16} = a + 15d = m$, we get $S_{31} = 31m$.
