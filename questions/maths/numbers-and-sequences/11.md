## In an A.P., the first term is $1$ and the common difference is $4$. How many terms of the A.P. must be taken for their sum to be equal to $120$?

---

#### Given $a = 1$, $d = 4$, and $S_n = 120$. Using $S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n-1)d]$:
$\frac{n}{2}[2(1) + 4(n-1)] = 120 \implies n(2n-1) = 120 \implies 2n^2 - n - 120 = 0$.
Factoring: $(n-8)(2n+15) = 0$. Since the number of terms $n$ must be a positive integer, $n = 8$.
