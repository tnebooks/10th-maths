## The next term of the sequence $\dfrac{3}{16}, \dfrac{1}{8}, \dfrac{1}{12}, \dfrac{1}{18}, \cdots$ is

---

#### Check the ratio of consecutive terms:
$\frac{t_2}{t_1} = \frac{1/8}{3/16} = \frac{2}{3}$, $\frac{t_3}{t_2} = \frac{1/12}{1/8} = \frac{2}{3}$, $\frac{t_4}{t_3} = \frac{1/18}{1/12} = \frac{2}{3}$.
This is a Geometric Progression with common ratio $r = \frac{2}{3}$. The next term is $t_5 = t_4 \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{1}{18} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{1}{27}$.
