## If the sequence $t_1, t_2, t_3, \ldots$ are in A.P. then the sequence $t_6, t_{12}, t_{18}, \ldots$ is

---

#### Let the original A.P. have common difference $d$. The terms are $t_6 = a + 5d$, $t_{12} = a + 11d$, and $t_{18} = a + 17d$. The difference between successive terms is $t_{12} - t_6 = 6d$ and $t_{18} - t_{12} = 6d$. Since the difference between consecutive terms is constant ($6d$), the new sequence is also an Arithmetic Progression.
