## Using Euclid's division lemma, if the cube of any positive integer is divided by $9$ then the possible remainders are

---

#### Any positive integer $n$ can be represented as $3k$, $3k+1$, or $3k+2$. Cubing these:
- $(3k)^3 = 27k^3 = 9(3k^3)$, remainder $0$.
- $(3k+1)^3 = 27k^3 + 27k^2 + 9k + 1 = 9m + 1$, remainder $1$.
- $(3k+2)^3 = 27k^3 + 54k^2 + 36k + 8 = 9m + 8$, remainder $8$.
Thus, dividing the cube of any positive integer by 9 yields remainders $0, 1,$ or $8$.
