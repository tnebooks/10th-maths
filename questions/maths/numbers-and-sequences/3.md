## If the HCF of $65$ and $117$ is expressible in the form of $65m - 117$, then the value of $m$ is

---

#### Using Euclid's algorithm or prime factorization, $65 = 13 \times 5$ and $117 = 13 \times 9$, so $\text{HCF}(65, 117) = 13$. We are given $65m - 117 = 13$. Adding $117$ to both sides gives $65m = 130 \implies m = \frac{130}{65} = 2$.
