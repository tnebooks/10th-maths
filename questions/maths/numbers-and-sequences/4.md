## The sum of the exponents of the prime factors in the prime factorization of $1729$ is

---

#### Prime factorizing $1729$: $1729 = 7 \times 13 \times 19 = 7^1 \times 13^1 \times 19^1$. The exponents of the prime factors are $1, 1,$ and $1$. Their sum is $1 + 1 + 1 = 3$.
