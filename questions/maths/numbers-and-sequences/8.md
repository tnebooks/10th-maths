## The first term of an arithmetic progression is unity and the common difference is $4$. Which of the following will be a term of this A.P.

---

#### The $n^{\text{th}}$ term of an A.P. is $t_n = a + (n-1)d = 1 + 4(n-1)$. Thus, $t_n - 1 = 4(n-1)$, meaning $t_n - 1$ must be divisible by 4:
- $4551 - 1 = 4550$ (not divisible by 4)
- $10091 - 1 = 10090$ (not divisible by 4)
- $7881 - 1 = 7880$, which is divisible by 4 ($7880/4 = 1970 \implies n = 1971$)
- $13531 - 1 = 13530$ (not divisible by 4).
Hence, $7881$ is a term of the A.P.
