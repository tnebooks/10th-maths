## If $6$ times of $6^{\text{th}}$ term of an A.P. is equal to $7$ times the $7^{\text{th}}$ term, then the $13^{\text{th}}$ term of the A.P. is

---

#### Given $6 \cdot t_6 = 7 \cdot t_7$. Substituting the formula for the $n^{\text{th}}$ term:
$6(a + 5d) = 7(a + 6d) \implies 6a + 30d = 7a + 42d \implies a + 12d = 0$.
The $13^{\text{th}}$ term is $t_{13} = a + 12d$. Since $a + 12d = 0$, $t_{13} = 0$.
