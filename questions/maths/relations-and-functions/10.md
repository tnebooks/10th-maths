## If $f(x) = 2x^2$ and $g(x) = \dfrac{1}{3x}$, then $f \circ g$ is

---

#### By definition of composite functions, $(f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x))$. Substituting $g(x) = \frac{1}{3x}$ into $f$, we get $f\left(\frac{1}{3x}\right) = 2\left(\frac{1}{3x}\right)^2 = 2 \times \frac{1}{9x^2} = \frac{2}{9x^2}$.
