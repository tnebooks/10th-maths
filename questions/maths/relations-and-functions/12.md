## Let $f$ and $g$ be two functions given by
$$f = \{(0,1),(2,0),(3,-4),(4,2),(5,7)\}$$
$$g = \{(0,2),(1,0),(2,4),(-4,2),(7,0)\}$$
then the range of $f \circ g$ is

---

#### Evaluate $(f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x))$ for each element in the domain of $g$:
- $g(0) = 2 \implies f(2) = 0$
- $g(1) = 0 \implies f(0) = 1$
- $g(2) = 4 \implies f(4) = 2$
- $g(-4) = 2 \implies f(2) = 0$
- $g(7) = 0 \implies f(0) = 1$
The set of distinct output values is $\{0, 1, 2\}$. Hence, the range of $f \circ g$ is $\{0, 1, 2\}$.
