---
choices:
  - $f(xy) = f(x).f(y)$
  - $f(xy) \geq f(x).f(y)$
  - None of these
answers:
  - $f(xy) \leq f(x).f(y)$
---

## Let $f(x) = \sqrt{1+x^2}$ then

---

#### We have $f(xy) = \sqrt{1 + x^2 y^2}$. Also, $f(x) \cdot f(y) = \sqrt{1+x^2}\sqrt{1+y^2} = \sqrt{1 + x^2 + y^2 + x^2 y^2}$. Since $x^2 \geq 0$ and $y^2 \geq 0$, it follows that $1 + x^2 y^2 \leq 1 + x^2 + y^2 + x^2 y^2$. Taking the square root on both sides yields $f(xy) \leq f(x) \cdot f(y)$.
