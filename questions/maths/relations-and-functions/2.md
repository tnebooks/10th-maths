## $A = \{a,b,p\}$, $B = \{2,3\}$, $C = \{p,q,r,s\}$ then $n[(A \cup C) \times B]$ is

---

#### $A \cup C = \{a,b,p\} \cup \{p,q,r,s\} = \{a,b,p,q,r,s\}$, so $n(A \cup C) = 6$. Since $B = \{2,3\}$, $n(B) = 2$. Therefore, $n[(A \cup C) \times B] = n(A \cup C) \times n(B) = 6 \times 2 = 12$.
