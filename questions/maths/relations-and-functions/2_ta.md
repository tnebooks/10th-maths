## \( A = \{a,b,p\}, B = \{2,3\}, C = \{p,q,r,s\} \) எனில், \( n[(A \cup C) \times B] \) ஆனது

---

#### \( A \cup C = \{a,b,p,q,r,s\} \) என்பதால், \( n(A \cup C) = 6 \) ஆகும். \( B = \{2,3\} \) என்பதால், \( n(B) = 2 \) ஆகும். எனவே, \( n[(A \cup C) \times B] = n(A \cup C) \times n(B) = 6 \times 2 = 12 \) ஆகும்.
