choices:
  - $(B \times D) \subset (A \times C)$
  - $(A \times B) \subset (A \times D)$
  - $(D \times A) \subset (B \times A)$
answers:
  - $(A \times C) \subset (B \times D)$
---

## If $A = \{1,2\}$, $B = \{1,2,3,4\}$, $C = \{5,6\}$ and $D = \{5,6,7,8\}$ then state which of the following statement is true.

---

#### Since $A = \{1,2\} \subset B = \{1,2,3,4\}$ and $C = \{5,6\} \subset D = \{5,6,7,8\}$, every ordered pair $(a,c) \in A \times C$ satisfies $a \in B$ and $c \in D$, meaning $(a,c) \in B \times D$. Hence, $(A \times C) \subset (B \times D)$ is the true statement.
