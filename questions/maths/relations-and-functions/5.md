## The range of the relation $R = \{(x,x^2) \mid x \text{ is a prime number less than } 13\}$ is

---

#### The prime numbers less than 13 are $2, 3, 5, 7,$ and $11$. The range of $R$ consists of the second elements of each ordered pair, which are the squares of these primes: $2^2 = 4$, $3^2 = 9$, $5^2 = 25$, $7^2 = 49$, and $11^2 = 121$. Thus, the range is $\{4, 9, 25, 49, 121\}$.
