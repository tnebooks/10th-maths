## If the ordered pairs $(a+2,4)$ and $(5,2a+b)$ are equal then $(a,b)$ is

---

#### Equating the corresponding components of the ordered pairs: $a + 2 = 5 \implies a = 3$. Next, $2a + b = 4 \implies 2(3) + b = 4 \implies 6 + b = 4 \implies b = -2$. Therefore, $(a,b) = (3,-2)$.
