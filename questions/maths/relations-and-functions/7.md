## Let $n(A) = m$ and $n(B) = n$ then the total number of non-empty relations that can be defined from $A$ to $B$ is

---

#### A relation from $A$ to $B$ is any subset of $A \times B$. Since $n(A \times B) = mn$, the total number of subsets (relations) is $2^{mn}$. Excluding the empty relation $\emptyset$, the number of non-empty relations is $2^{mn} - 1$.
