## Let $A = \{1,2,3,4\}$ and $B = \{4,8,9,10\}$. A function $f:A\to B$ given by $f = \{(1,4),(2,8),(3,9),(4,10)\}$ is a

---

#### Distinct elements in domain $A$ have distinct images in co-domain $B$: $f(1)=4, f(2)=8, f(3)=9, f(4)=10$. Since no two elements in $A$ share the same image, $f$ is a one-to-one function.
