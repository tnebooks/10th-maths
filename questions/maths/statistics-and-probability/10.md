## The probability a red marble selected at random from a jar containing $p$ red, $q$ blue and $r$ green marbles is

---

#### The total number of outcomes is the total number of marbles in the jar:
$$n(S) = p + q + r$$
The number of favorable outcomes for choosing a red marble is:
$$n(\text{Red}) = p$$
Therefore, the probability is:
$$P(\text{Red}) = \frac{n(\text{Red})}{n(S)} = \frac{p}{p+q+r}$$
