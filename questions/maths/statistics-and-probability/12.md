## The probability of getting a job for a person is $\dfrac{x}{3}$. If the probability of not getting the job is $\dfrac{2}{3}$ then the value of $x$ is

---

#### The sum of the probability of an event and its complementary event is 1:
$$P(\text{getting job}) + P(\text{not getting job}) = 1$$
$$\frac{x}{3} + \frac{2}{3} = 1 \implies \frac{x + 2}{3} = 1 \implies x + 2 = 3 \implies x = 1$$
