## Kamalam went to play a lucky draw contest. 135 tickets of the lucky draw were sold. If the probability of Kamalam winning is $\dfrac{1}{9}$, then the number of tickets bought by Kamalam is

---

#### Let the number of tickets bought by Kamalam be $k$.
The total number of tickets sold is $n(S) = 135$.
The probability of winning is:
$$P(\text{winning}) = \frac{k}{135}$$
Given that $P(\text{winning}) = \frac{1}{9}$:
$$\frac{k}{135} = \frac{1}{9} \implies k = \frac{135}{9} = 15$$
