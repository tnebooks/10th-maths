## If a letter is chosen at random from the English alphabets $\{a,b,\ldots,z\}$, then the probability that the letter chosen precedes $x$

---

#### The English alphabet has 26 letters, so $n(S) = 26$.
The letter $x$ is the 24th letter of the alphabet.
The letters that precede $x$ are the first 23 letters ($a$ through $w$):
$$n(E) = 23$$
Therefore, the probability is:
$$P(E) = \frac{n(E)}{n(S)} = \frac{23}{26}$$
