## The sum of all deviations of the data from its mean is

---

#### Let the data values be $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n$ with arithmetic mean $\bar{x} = \frac{\sum x_i}{n}$.
The sum of deviations of all values from the mean is:
$$\sum_{i=1}^n (x_i - \bar{x}) = \sum_{i=1}^n x_i - n\bar{x} = n\bar{x} - n\bar{x} = 0$$
Therefore, the algebraic sum of deviations about the mean is always zero.
