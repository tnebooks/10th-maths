## The mean of 100 observations is 40 and their standard deviation is 3. The sum of squares of all observations is

---

#### Given $n = 100$, mean $\bar{x} = 40$, and standard deviation $\sigma = 3$.
The formula relating standard deviation and sum of squares is:
$$\sigma^2 = \frac{\sum x_i^2}{n} - (\bar{x})^2$$
Substituting the given values:
$$3^2 = \frac{\sum x_i^2}{100} - 40^2 \implies 9 = \frac{\sum x_i^2}{100} - 1600$$
$$\frac{\sum x_i^2}{100} = 1609 \implies \sum x_i^2 = 1609 \times 100 = 160900$$
