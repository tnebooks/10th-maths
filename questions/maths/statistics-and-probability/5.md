## Variance of first 20 natural numbers is

---

#### The formula for the variance of the first $n$ natural numbers is:
$$\sigma^2 = \frac{n^2 - 1}{12}$$
For $n = 20$:
$$\sigma^2 = \frac{20^2 - 1}{12} = \frac{400 - 1}{12} = \frac{399}{12} = 33.25$$
