## The standard deviation of a data is 3. If each value is multiplied by 5 then the new variance is

---

#### When each observation of a dataset is multiplied by a constant $k = 5$, the new standard deviation becomes $k$ times the original standard deviation:
$$\sigma_{\text{new}} = 5 \times 3 = 15$$
The new variance is the square of the new standard deviation:
$$\text{Variance}_{\text{new}} = (\sigma_{\text{new}})^2 = 15^2 = 225$$
