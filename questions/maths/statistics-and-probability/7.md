## If the standard deviation of $x,y,z$ is $p$ then the standard deviation of $3x+5$, $3y+5$, $3z+5$ is

---

#### Standard deviation is independent of change of origin (adding or subtracting a constant) but depends on change of scale (multiplying by a constant).
If each observation $x_i$ is transformed to $a x_i + b$, the new standard deviation is $|a| \times \sigma$.
Here, $a = 3$ and $b = 5$, and original standard deviation $\sigma = p$:
$$\sigma_{\text{new}} = |3| \times p = 3p$$
