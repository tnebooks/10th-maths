## If the mean and coefficient of variation of a data are 4 and 87.5% then the standard deviation is

---

#### The formula for the coefficient of variation (C.V.) is:
$$\text{C.V.} = \frac{\sigma}{\bar{x}} \times 100\%$$
Given $\bar{x} = 4$ and $\text{C.V.} = 87.5\%$:
$$87.5 = \frac{\sigma}{4} \times 100 \implies \frac{\sigma}{4} = \frac{87.5}{100} = 0.875 \implies \sigma = 4 \times 0.875 = 3.5$$
