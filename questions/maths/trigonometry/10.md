## If the ratio of the height of a tower and the length of its shadow is $\sqrt{3} : 1$, then the angle of elevation of the sun has measure

---

#### Let the height of the tower be $h$ and the length of its shadow be $s$.
Given that $\dfrac{h}{s} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{1}$.
If $\theta$ is the angle of elevation of the sun:
$$\tan\theta = \frac{\text{height}}{\text{shadow length}} = \frac{h}{s} = \sqrt{3}$$
Since $\tan 60^\circ = \sqrt{3}$, the angle of elevation is $\theta = 60^\circ$.
