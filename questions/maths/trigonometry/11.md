## The electric pole subtends an angle of $30^\circ$ at a point on the same level as its foot. At a second point '$b$' metres above the first, the depression of the foot of the pole is $60^\circ$. The height of the pole (in metres) is equal to

---

#### Let the height of the pole be $h$ and the horizontal distance from the foot of the pole to the first point $A$ be $d$.
From the first point $A$ on the ground:
$$\tan 30^\circ = \frac{h}{d} \implies \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{h}{d} \implies d = h\sqrt{3}$$
At the second point $B$, located vertically $b$ metres above $A$, the angle of depression of the foot of the pole is $60^\circ$:
$$\tan 60^\circ = \frac{b}{d} \implies \sqrt{3} = \frac{b}{d} \implies d = \frac{b}{\sqrt{3}}$$
Equating the two expressions for $d$:
$$h\sqrt{3} = \frac{b}{\sqrt{3}} \implies h = \frac{b}{3}$$
