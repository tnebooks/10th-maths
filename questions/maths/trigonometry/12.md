## A tower is 60 m heigh. Its shadow reduces by $x$ metres when the angle of elevation of the sun increases from $30^\circ$ to $45^\circ$ then $x$ is equal to

---

#### Let the height of the tower be $h = 60\text{ m}$.
When the angle of elevation is $45^\circ$, the shadow length is:
$$s_1 = \frac{h}{\tan 45^\circ} = \frac{60}{1} = 60\text{ m}$$
When the angle of elevation is $30^\circ$, the shadow length is:
$$s_2 = \frac{h}{\tan 30^\circ} = \frac{60}{1/\sqrt{3}} = 60\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$$
The reduction in the length of shadow is $x = s_2 - s_1$:
$$x = 60\sqrt{3} - 60 = 60(\sqrt{3} - 1) = 60(1.732 - 1) = 60 \times 0.732 = 43.92\text{ m}$$
