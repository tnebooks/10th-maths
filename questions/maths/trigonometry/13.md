## The angle of depression of the top and bottom of 20 m tall building from the top of a multistoried building are $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$ respectively. The height of the multistoried building and the distance between two buildings (in metres) is

---

#### Let the height of the multistoried building be $H$ and the distance between the two buildings be $d$.
The height of the smaller building is $h = 20\text{ m}$.
From the top of the multistoried building:
- Angle of depression to the top of the 20 m building is $30^\circ$:
$$\tan 30^\circ = \frac{H - 20}{d} \implies \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{H - 20}{d} \implies d = \sqrt{3}(H - 20)$$
- Angle of depression to the bottom of the 20 m building is $60^\circ$:
$$\tan 60^\circ = \frac{H}{d} \implies \sqrt{3} = \frac{H}{d} \implies d = \frac{H}{\sqrt{3}}$$
Equating the two expressions for $d$:
$$\frac{H}{\sqrt{3}} = \sqrt{3}(H - 20) \implies H = 3(H - 20) = 3H - 60 \implies 2H = 60 \implies H = 30\text{ m}$$
Now finding $d$:
$$d = \frac{30}{\sqrt{3}} = 10\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$$
Thus, the height is $30\text{ m}$ and the distance is $10\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$.
