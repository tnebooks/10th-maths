answers:

## Two persons are standing '$x$' metres apart from each other and the height of the first person is double that of the other. If from the middle point of the line joining their feet an observer finds the angular elevations of their tops to be complementary, then the height of the shorter person (in metres) is

---

#### Let the height of the shorter person be $h$, then the taller person has height $2h$.
The distance between them is $x$, so the distance from the midpoint to each person is $\frac{x}{2}$.
Let the angles of elevation from the midpoint to their tops be $\theta_1$ and $\theta_2$:
$$\tan\theta_1 = \frac{h}{x/2} = \frac{2h}{x}$$
$$\tan\theta_2 = \frac{2h}{x/2} = \frac{4h}{x}$$
Since the angles are complementary, $\theta_1 + \theta_2 = 90^\circ$, which means $\tan\theta_1 \times \tan\theta_2 = 1$:
$$\left(\frac{2h}{x}\right)\left(\frac{4h}{x}\right) = 1 \implies \frac{8h^2}{x^2} = 1 \implies h^2 = \frac{x^2}{8} \implies h = \frac{x}{\sqrt{8}} = \frac{x}{2\sqrt{2}}$$
