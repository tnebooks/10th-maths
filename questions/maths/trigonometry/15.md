## The angle of elevation of a cloud from a point $h$ metres above a lake is $\beta$. The angle of depression of its reflection in the lake is $45^\circ$. The height of location of the cloud from the lake is

---

#### Let the height of the cloud above the surface of the lake be $H$.
The point of observation $P$ is at height $h$ above the lake.
- The vertical distance from $P$ to the cloud is $H - h$.
- The reflection of the cloud is at a depth $H$ below the lake surface, so the vertical distance from $P$ to the reflection is $H + h$.
Let the horizontal distance from $P$ to the cloud be $d$.
From the angle of elevation $\beta$:
$$\tan\beta = \frac{H - h}{d} \implies d = \frac{H - h}{\tan\beta}$$
From the angle of depression of the reflection ($45^\circ$):
$$\tan 45^\circ = \frac{H + h}{d} \implies 1 = \frac{H + h}{d} \implies d = H + h$$
Equating both expressions for $d$:
$$H + h = \frac{H - h}{\tan\beta} \implies (H + h)\tan\beta = H - h$$
$$H\tan\beta + h\tan\beta = H - h \implies h(1 + \tan\beta) = H(1 - \tan\beta)$$
$$H = \frac{h(1 + \tan\beta)}{1 - \tan\beta}$$
