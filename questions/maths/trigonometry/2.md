## $\tan\theta\,\text{cosec}^2\theta - \tan\theta$ is equal to

---

#### Factoring out $\tan\theta$:
$$\tan\theta\,\text{cosec}^2\theta - \tan\theta = \tan\theta(\text{cosec}^2\theta - 1)$$
Using the identity $\text{cosec}^2\theta - 1 = \cot^2\theta$ and $\tan\theta\cot\theta = 1$:
$$= \tan\theta \cdot \cot^2\theta = \tan\theta \cdot \cot\theta \cdot \cot\theta = 1 \cdot \cot\theta = \cot\theta$$
