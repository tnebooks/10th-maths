## If $5x = \sec\theta$ and $\dfrac{5}{y} = \tan\theta$, then $x^2 - \dfrac{1}{y^2}$ is equal to

---

#### From the given equations:
$$x = \frac{\sec\theta}{5} \implies x^2 = \frac{\sec^2\theta}{25}$$
$$\frac{1}{y} = \frac{\tan\theta}{5} \implies \frac{1}{y^2} = \frac{\tan^2\theta}{25}$$
Subtracting the two terms:
$$x^2 - \frac{1}{y^2} = \frac{\sec^2\theta - \tan^2\theta}{25}$$
Using the trigonometric identity $\sec^2\theta - \tan^2\theta = 1$:
$$x^2 - \frac{1}{y^2} = \frac{1}{25}$$
