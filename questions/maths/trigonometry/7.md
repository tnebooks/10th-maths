  - $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2} + \dfrac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$
  - $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2} - \dfrac{y^2}{b^2} = 0$
answers:
  - $\dfrac{y^2}{b^2} - \dfrac{x^2}{a^2} = 1$

## If $x = a\tan\theta$ and $y = b\sec\theta$ then

---

#### From the given relations:
$$\tan\theta = \frac{x}{a} \implies \tan^2\theta = \frac{x^2}{a^2}$$
$$\sec\theta = \frac{y}{b} \implies \sec^2\theta = \frac{y^2}{b^2}$$
Using the standard trigonometric identity $\sec^2\theta - \tan^2\theta = 1$:
$$\frac{y^2}{b^2} - \frac{x^2}{a^2} = 1$$
