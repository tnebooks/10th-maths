## $a\cot\theta + b\,\text{cosec}\,\theta = p$ and $b\cot\theta + a\,\text{cosec}\,\theta = q$ then $p^2 - q^2$ is equal to

---

#### Squaring $p$ and $q$:
$$p^2 = a^2\cot^2\theta + b^2\text{cosec}^2\theta + 2ab\cot\theta\,\text{cosec}\,\theta$$
$$q^2 = b^2\cot^2\theta + a^2\text{cosec}^2\theta + 2ab\cot\theta\,\text{cosec}\,\theta$$
Subtracting $q^2$ from $p^2$:
$$p^2 - q^2 = (a^2 - b^2)\cot^2\theta + (b^2 - a^2)\text{cosec}^2\theta$$
$$= (b^2 - a^2)\text{cosec}^2\theta - (b^2 - a^2)\cot^2\theta = (b^2 - a^2)(\text{cosec}^2\theta - \cot^2\theta)$$
Since $\text{cosec}^2\theta - \cot^2\theta = 1$:
$$p^2 - q^2 = b^2 - a^2$$
